Course Syllabus • 10 Core Units

What You Will Study in This Course

The UGC NET Forensic Science December 2026 programme is structured around these ten comprehensive units, taught with in-depth concept clarity, previous-year question mastery, and expert mentorship.

UNIT 01

Fundamentals of Forensic Science

Historical development, principles, crime scene investigation protocols, physical evidence handling, chain of custody, and legal frameworks under IPC, CrPC & Indian Evidence Act.

Crime Scene Management Physical Evidence IPC & CrPC Provisions Chain of Custody
UNIT 02

Analytical Techniques / Instrumentation

Theoretical foundations and forensic applications of Microscopy (optical, SEM, TEM), Chromatography (TLC, GC, HPLC), Spectroscopy (UV-Vis, FTIR, AAS, MS), and Electrophoresis.

Microscopy (SEM & TEM) Spectroscopy (FTIR & AAS) Chromatography (GC & HPLC) Electrophoresis
UNIT 03

Forensic Biology, Serology & DNA

Identification and examination of biological fluids (blood, semen, saliva), bloodstain pattern analysis, forensic serology, DNA isolation, PCR, STR typing, forensic botany, and entomology.

Biological Fluid Analysis DNA Profiling & STR Bloodstain Patterns Forensic Entomology
UNIT 04

Forensic Chemistry & Toxicology

Analysis of illicit drugs under the NDPS Act, extraction and identification of poisons, heavy metals, alcohol metabolism, petroleum products, cosmetics, dyes, and chemical adulterants.

Narcotics & NDPS Act Toxicology & Poisons Alcohol Estimation Petroleum Products
UNIT 05

Forensic Ballistics

Classification of firearms and ammunition, internal, external and terminal ballistics, bullet and cartridge case comparison, firing marks, range of firing, and Gunshot Residue (GSR) analysis.

Firearms Classification Ballistic Mechanics GSR & Range Estimation Striation Analysis
UNIT 06

Fire, Explosives & Photography

Arson and fire debris investigation, classification and analysis of primary and secondary explosives, post-blast scene examination, crime scene photography, videography, and alternate light sources.

Arson & Accelerants Explosives Chemistry Post-Blast Examination Forensic Photography
UNIT 07

Trace Evidence & Digital Forensics

Forensic analysis of glass, soil, paint, fibers, and hairs. Digital evidence acquisition, mobile forensics, data recovery, file system analysis, and cyber crime investigation under the IT Act.

Trace Analysis (Glass & Paint) Digital Evidence Seizure Mobile Forensics IT Act & Cyber Laws
UNIT 08

Fingerprints & Biometrics

Principles of friction ridge skin, pattern types (loops, whorls, arches), development of latent prints using physical and chemical methods, Automated Fingerprint ID Systems (AFIS), and biometric modalities.

Ridge Patterns & Minutiae Latent Print Development AFIS Databases Facial & Iris Biometrics
UNIT 09

Questioned Documents

Scientific principles of handwriting examination, signature verification, detection of forgery and alterations, examination of printed documents, photocopiers, ink and paper analysis, and counterfeit currency.

Handwriting Identification Forgery Detection Ink & Paper Analysis Counterfeit Currency
UNIT 10

Forensic Medicine, Anthropology & Odontology

Medicolegal autopsy, post-mortem changes, time since death estimation, mechanical injuries, wounds, identification of skeletal remains, stature estimation, age/sex determination, and bite mark analysis.

Post-Mortem Thanatology Time Since Death Skeletal Anthropology Forensic Odontology
